What we screen out

We do not list properties near any of these, however cheap the reserve.

How it works

  1. You pay ₹2,750 + GST for the premium property you choose.
  2. We do the background check and visit the property. Latest EC and papers from the bank, and the latest verified actual real site visit photographs and videos.
  3. We verify everything. Then we enable your features and confirm by email, WhatsApp and SMS, all within 48 hours.

Your unlock includes every Pro feature for that property, including an advocate’s time reserved with payment.
