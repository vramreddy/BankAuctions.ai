Questions, answered.

The short version of how BankAuctions.ai works.

Are you an auctioneer or agent?

No. We provide intelligence and data on auction properties and prices. Bids are placed with the bank or on its e-auction portal, and we do not act as an intermediary.

What is the AI Rank?

Each listing gets a score out of 100 from discount to market (up to 40), possession status (25), location and liquidity (20), and documents and photos (15). It is then ranked only within its own category and city, so a plot is never compared with a warehouse.

What is the difference between Pro and Premium?

Pro is a one-off ₹499 pack that unlocks 5 standard properties in full (AI rank breakdown, field photographs, valuation summary). While you have unlocks left, Ask in your words and map search are on too. Premium is a separate plan that opens a curated list of highway, corner and high-commercial-value listings for one city.

Where do the photographs come from?

Some listings carry photographs released by the bank, marked Bank supplied. Where a bank supplies none, we show a representative image and label it. Photographs taken on site by our field verification engineers are available on paid plans.

Why is the lender hidden on Premium properties?

Premium properties are the most sought-after, so free and Pro users see the price and a distance from a known landmark, for example “About 3 km from Tidel Park”, instead of the lender and exact locality. The Premium plan for that city shows the lender, locality and field photographs.

Can I share a property with someone?

Yes, with a Pro pack or a Premium plan. Use the share icons on the property page for Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, X or email. Free users can browse but not share.

Which cities do you cover?

Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Coimbatore and Kerala today. More cities are planned.

How current is the data?

Listings come from bank sale notices and are refreshed daily in the live product. This prototype uses illustrative data.

Does my Pro pack expire?

No. A Pro pack costs ₹499 for 5 properties and has no time limit. It ends only when you have unlocked 5 properties. Every property you unlock is kept in My archive, which you can open any time you sign in.

Can I cancel Premium?

Premium is time-limited (1, 2 or 3 months) and does not renew automatically in this prototype; final billing terms are yet to be confirmed.
